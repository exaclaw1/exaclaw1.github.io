Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application ("the App") handles information in connection with publishing video content to TikTok.

1. Information We Access

The App accesses only the information required to publish video content to the TikTok account that has explicitly authorized it, such as basic account identifiers and upload/publishing permissions granted through TikTok's official OAuth authorization flow.

2. How Information Is Used

Access granted by the account owner is used solely to schedule and publish video content to that account. The App does not sell, rent, or share any account information with third parties.

3. Data Storage

Authorization tokens are stored securely and are used only for the purpose of publishing content on behalf of the authorizing account. No content or account data is retained beyond what is necessary for the App's publishing function.

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services (such as media hosting or processing tools) solely to prepare video files before publishing. These services do not receive TikTok account credentials.

5. Revoking Access

The account owner may revoke the App's access at any time through TikTok's account settings.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer through the GitHub repository associated with this page.
